### What I do

- 🛍️ **Shopify development** · Shopify Partner. Custom Liquid themes, Hydrogen headless storefronts and B2B buying flows, plus performance and tracking that matches your orders.
- 🧠 **AI integration** · Retrieval-augmented assistants that answer from your documents and show their sources, and LLM features inside products you already have.
- ⚛️ **React and Next.js** · Web apps, MVPs and admin tools, with Node.js, NestJS, PostgreSQL or MongoDB behind them.
- 📈 **Analytics and tracking** · GA4 and Google Tag Manager set up properly, including single-page apps and privacy-sensitive products.

I work as **ANODA Loop**: AI agents draft code, tests and docs, and I decide, review and sign off. Nothing ships without my review. [How it works →](https://anoda.ba/how-i-work/anoda-loop)
